EP

When It Binds

Blueberry Records

26.02.2021

When It Binds is a collection of songs composed over 3 years, and serves as Bénédicte’s personal exploration of what happens after an emotional connection is established with someone. How does this change who we are? How are our emotional landscapes altered with this new form of inter-subjectivity? Each song on the album is a different and searching sonic interpretation of these questions and a response to ever-changing emotional shifts.

  • Written, produced and mixed by Bénédicte
  • Production on "Drift" by Bénédicte and XGLARE

Through field recordings and severed vocal samples, she creates a restrained world, rife with feeling, and lifted skyward with soaring ambient columns. Coming across her music is like finding an entirely new species in your backyard. — Daryl Keating for Exclaim!

Bénédicte is the musical project of Montreal-based producer and sound artist Maxime Gordon. Combining soaring synths, distorted field recordings and glittering vocal samples, her music explores moments of introspection and feeling. She has performed at MUTEK, SAT, AKOUSMA and PHI in Montreal, as well as Punctum (Prague) and CULTVR (Wales), and has shared bills with ML Buch, Malibu and BABii.

She has composed spatial sound works for large-scale speaker systems like 4DSOUND, the SAT Satosphère and AKOUSMA’s acousmonium, and developed this practice through residencies with PRIM x Codes d’accès, CULTVR and the Spatial Sound Institute (Budapest). She also writes music for contemporary dance and cinema.

Her 2021 EP When It Binds was released on Blueberry Records. She is now working on its follow-up with support from the Canada Council for the Arts.
